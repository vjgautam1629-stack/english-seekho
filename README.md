# हिंदी से English सीखें

A free app for Hindi speakers to learn English sentence by sentence. Read a Hindi sentence, try making it in English, then check your answer.

**[Open the app](https://vjgautam1629-stack.github.io/english-seekho/)**

## Features

- 3,932 Hindi and English practice sentences
- Show or hide the English translation
- Sentence patterns and word-by-word grammar explanations
- English pronunciation using your device's text-to-speech
- Search by Hindi, English, or sentence pattern
- Filter by sentence pattern or part of speech
- Mark sentences as complete or save them for review
- Track your progress
- Works offline after your first visit
- Installs on your phone or computer
- Swipe gestures on mobile and keyboard shortcuts on desktop

## Install the app

**Android:** Open the app link in Chrome, tap **⋮**, then tap **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.

**iPhone or iPad:** Open the app link in Safari, tap **Share**, then tap **Add to Home Screen**.

**Computer:** Open the app link in Chrome or Edge, then click the install icon in the address bar if it appears.

## How to use it

1. Open a Hindi sentence.
2. Try making the sentence in English.
3. Tap **English दिखाएं** to check your answer.
4. Tap **Pattern दिखाएं** to see the sentence structure and grammar.
5. Mark the sentence as complete or save it for review.

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `E` | Show or hide English |
| `P` | Show or hide the pattern |
| `C` | Mark as complete |
| `R` | Save for review |
| `←` / `→` | Previous or next sentence |
| `Esc` | Go back |
| `H` | Go to Home |

## Your data

Your progress is saved only in your browser on your device. It doesn't sync to other devices, and clearing your browser data may delete it.
